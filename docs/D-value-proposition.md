# D. Key Value Proposition

## D.1 The headline

> **Run your entire institute from one screen.**
> Registration, classes, tuition, attendance, and communication — connected, automated, and
> visible. Stop managing your institute from a spreadsheet and a WhatsApp group.

Supporting line for the hero:

> Talkora gives your institute a private workspace where every student, class, payment,
> and message lives in one place — so your staff stop doing data entry and start doing work
> that grows the business.

## D.2 The eight core value claims

Each claim below is written the way it should appear in marketing, followed by the mechanism
that makes it true and the proof point that makes it credible. **Never ship a claim without
the mechanism.** Vague benefit statements are what makes education SaaS marketing worthless.

---

### 1. Centralise operations
**Claim:** One workspace replaces your spreadsheets, notebooks, and group chats.
**Mechanism:** A single tenant data model where student, enrolment, payment, attendance,
grade, and message all reference the same records — so any question spanning two of them is
answerable instantly.
**Proof:** "Which students with unpaid balances attended last week?" — one filter, zero
cross-referencing.

### 2. Reduce administrative work
**Claim:** Save 15–25 hours of admin work per staff member per month.
**Mechanism:** Automation of the four highest-volume manual tasks: payment reminders,
absence notifications, schedule distribution, and term re-enrolment offers. Plus student
self-service, which removes the routine phone calls entirely.
**Proof:** Reminder automation alone replaces ~200 manual messages per month at 400 students.

### 3. Look more professional
**Claim:** Your institute communicates like a serious organisation.
**Mechanism:** Branded SMS and email, automatic receipts and invoices, a student portal with
your name and logo, PDF progress reports, and digital certificates.
**Proof:** Students receive a schedule link and a receipt within 60 seconds of enrolling —
before they leave the building.

### 4. Speed up registration and payment
**Claim:** Enrol a student in under two minutes and get paid on the spot.
**Mechanism:** Lead → placement test → level → available classes → enrolment → instalment
plan → payment link, as one continuous flow with no re-typing. Online payment for those who
want it, cash and receipt-upload for those who don't.
**Proof:** Median enrolment completion time under 120 seconds, measured in-product.

### 5. Make communication effortless and reliable
**Claim:** Reach the right students automatically, and know they received it.
**Mechanism:** Audience targeting by class, term, level, branch, or balance status; templated
messages with variables; delivery logs per recipient; automated triggers on absence, due
date, and schedule change.
**Proof:** Every message has a delivery record. "We told them" stops being a matter of opinion.

### 6. Improve student experience and retention
**Claim:** Students who can see their schedule, balance, and progress re-enrol more.
**Mechanism:** Self-service portal, proactive notifications, visible progress tracking, and a
one-tap re-enrolment offer at term end with their history pre-filled and loyalty pricing applied.
**Proof:** Retention becomes a number the institute can see per term, per class, per teacher —
usually for the first time in its history.

### 7. Give managers real insight
**Claim:** Know what's happening in your institute without asking anyone.
**Mechanism:** Live KPIs on the overview, term-over-term trends, per-branch comparison,
teacher workload, class fill rate, lead conversion, collection rate, and an at-risk student list.
**Proof:** Underfilled classes are flagged 10 days before term start — while there's still
time to merge, move, or market.

### 8. Grow revenue
**Claim:** Collect more of what you're already owed, and lose fewer students.
**Mechanism:** Three levers — (a) automated collection on overdue balances, (b) a structured
re-enrolment pipeline instead of hoping students come back, (c) lead follow-up tracking so
enquiries don't evaporate.
**Proof:** Institutes typically discover 5–15% of expected term tuition is uncollected. The
recovered portion usually exceeds the subscription cost by a wide margin.

## D.3 Value proposition by role

Different people need to hear different things. The demo script should branch on who's in
the room.

### To the owner
> "You'll know your real numbers — collected vs. expected, retention per term, which branch
> and which teacher is actually performing — without asking a single person for a report.
> And the money you're currently losing to uncollected tuition will more than cover the cost."

Emotional driver: **control.** They are tired of not knowing.

### To the academic manager
> "Term setup goes from two weeks of spreadsheet work to an afternoon. Clone the last term,
> adjust dates, assign teachers with conflicts checked automatically, publish. Underfilled
> classes flag themselves before it's too late to fix."

Emotional driver: **relief from the term-rollover crunch.** This is their worst month.

### To the front desk
> "Everything about a student is on one screen. Register, take a payment, print a receipt,
> send their schedule — without opening another file or asking anyone a question."

Emotional driver: **not looking incompetent in front of a waiting parent.**

### To the teacher
> "Open it on your phone. See today's classes. Mark attendance in ten seconds. Post homework
> once instead of messaging fifteen people. That's it — we're not adding to your workload."

Emotional driver: **suspicion that this is more work.** Defuse it immediately and explicitly.

### To the student
> "Your schedule, your balance, your homework, your grades — on your phone, without calling
> anyone. Pay online. Re-enrol next term in one tap."

Emotional driver: **not being left in the dark.**

## D.4 Why Talkora instead of the alternatives

| Alternative | Their position | Our counter |
|---|---|---|
| **Spreadsheets** | Free, familiar, flexible | Can't notify, enforce, or connect. Free until you count the hours and the uncollected tuition. We import your existing sheet on day one — you don't start over. |
| **Generic school ERP** | Feature-complete, established | Built for K-12. You'll pay for a parent portal, grade levels, and a curriculum module you'll never use, and still won't have terms, levels, placement tests, or instalments modelled properly. |
| **LMS (Moodle / Classroom)** | Free, good at content | Doesn't know what a payment is. Doesn't know a class has a capacity. It's the classroom, not the institute. Use it alongside us, not instead. |
| **CRM (Zoho / HubSpot)** | Great pipeline management | Models the sale, not the delivery. Your leads will be tidy and your term will still be chaos. |
| **Custom-built software** | Exactly what you asked for | Exactly what you asked for *18 months ago*, and the developer is unreachable. No updates, no security patches, no roadmap, and it breaks when a browser updates. |
| **Doing nothing** | Zero cost, zero risk | The cost is real and recurring — it's just invisible because it's spread across everyone's day. |

## D.5 The economic argument (the slide that closes deals)

For a 400-student institute:

| Line | Conservative estimate |
|---|---|
| Admin hours recovered | 2 staff × 18 h/month = 36 h/month |
| Value of recovered hours | 36 h × local admin rate |
| Uncollected tuition recovered | 4% of term revenue (of a typical 8–12% leak) |
| Students retained by re-enrolment flow | +3% term-over-term retention |
| Leads converted that would have been lost | +5–10 enrolments/term |
| **Against:** annual subscription | Growth-tier annual price |

The honest framing: **the subscription is usually recovered by the collections improvement
alone**, and everything else is upside. Don't oversell the hours saved — institute owners
discount time-savings claims heavily. Lead with money they're already owed but haven't collected.
That number is verifiable within their own records during the demo, which makes it credible.

## D.6 Objection handling

| Objection | Response |
|---|---|
| "My staff won't use it." | Correct concern — that's why teacher attendance takes 10 seconds on a phone and front desk tasks are single-screen. We onboard your staff, not just your data. Also: measure it. Adoption is visible in the admin dashboard, so you'll know in week two, not month six. |
| "We already have a system." | Keep it. Start with one branch and one term. If enrolments in Talkora aren't above 90% by term end, don't renew. |
| "It's too expensive." | Compare against the tuition you didn't collect last term. We'll pull that number together during the demo from your own records. |
| "Our data is sensitive." | Isolated tenant, encrypted at rest, role-scoped access, full audit log, and your data exportable in full at any time — including on the day you leave. |
| "We're too small." | Then you're on the Starter plan and setup takes an afternoon. The complexity scales with you. |
| "What if you disappear?" | Full data export in open formats, always available, no negotiation. Written into the contract. |
| "Our students won't use a portal." | They don't have to install anything. It's a link in an SMS that opens to the page they need, and they log in with their phone number. Anything they don't do, your front desk does as before. |

## D.7 The positioning sentence to protect

> **Talkora is the operating system for language institutes.**

Not "school management software" (K-12 association, commoditised, price-shopped).
Not "an LMS" (wrong category, wrong competitors, wrong buyer).
Not "a CRM for education" (undersells the operational core).

Every piece of copy, every screen, and every sales conversation should reinforce **operating
system**: the thing the business runs on, not a tool it occasionally uses.
